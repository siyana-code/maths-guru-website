# Sources

**Compiled: 9 October 2026**

Every source used in this research folder, grouped by what it was used for.

---

## How to read this

**Check dates.** Sri Lankan education policy moves, exam dates change, prices
change. Anything older than a year should be treated cautiously.

**We prefer official sources.** Where an official source and a news report
disagreed, we flagged the disagreement rather than picking the convenient one.

**Some sources are weak.** Where a number comes from a self-reported salary site
or an SEO blog, the document it supports says so.

### NIE availability caveat

**English-medium guides download freely. Sinhala and Tamil versions appear
unavailable** - the pages list grade options but returned no files for Grade 6
or 11 mathematics when queried.

This matters because NIE publishes maths vocabulary in English, and teachers
build on the English terms even in Sinhala and Tamil classrooms. **Check before
promising trilingual content aligned to NIE terminology.**

### The Sri Lankan study - the most relevant single source

**[Weeraratne & Chin (2018)](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1201489)** · [full PDF](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/EJ1201489.pdf)

Khan Academy in Sri Lankan Grade 9 maths. 632 treatment students. **+0.20 SD.**

> Critically: this worked **only because schools scheduled it into 2-3 of 5
> weekly maths periods.** Student choice alone did not produce this.
