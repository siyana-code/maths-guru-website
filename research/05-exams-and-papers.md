**Verified: 9 October 2026, against official Department of Examinations timetables**

How the O-Level Maths exam actually works, when it runs, and what past papers
are worth.

## When the O-Level exam runs

### December is the standard slot

O/L has traditionally been held in **December**. It is being restored to that
slot now after several disrupted years.

| Exam year | Held | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2019 | December 2019 | Normal |
| 2020 | March 2021 | COVID-19 |
| 2021 | Feb 2022, then May 2022 | COVID and repeated postponement |
| 2022 | August-September 2022 | Economic crisis |
| 2023 | Postponed to May 2024 | Economic crisis |
| 2024 | 17-26 March 2025 | Still recovering |
| 2025 | 17-26 February 2026 | Still recovering |
| **2026** | **8-17 December 2026** | **Normal December slot restored** |
| 2027 | 7-17 December 2027 | Confirmed |

**This is a restoration, not a new policy.** The Ministry of Education framed
the return to December as *"a significant return to normalcy"* after COVID and
the economic crisis. There is no announcement of a permanent change.

### Maths in the 2026 exam

From the official timetable, day 6:

```
Monday, 14 December 2026
  Mathematics Paper I    08:30 - 10:30    (2 hours)
  Mathematics Paper II   13:00 - 16:10    (3 hours 10 minutes)
```

The February 2026 timetable has an identical structure, only the date changed.

### When results come

**Roughly 3-4 months after the exam.** There is no single fixed month.

| Exam | Results published |
| --- | --- |
| 2024 exam (March 2025) | 11 July 2025 |
| 2025 exam (February 2026) | 19 June 2026 |

A December 2026 exam points to results around **March or April 2027**. No date
announced yet.

### The school year

From Ministry of Education Circular 30/2025, roughly:

| Term | Runs |
| --- | --- |
| First term | 1 January - 13 February |
| (holiday) | 14 February - 2 March |
| First term, second phase | 3 March - 10 April |
| (holiday) | 11 - 19 April |
| **Second term** | **20 April - 24 July** |
| Third term | Late July - 7 August, and 7 September - 4 December |

Then roughly four weeks off before the O-Level exam.

> **Treat the exact phase boundaries as provisional.** The circular was amended
> mid-year and reporting on the revision conflicts. Check the current version.

Also note **1 January is New Year's Day**, so the real first school day is
probably 5 January.

## The structure of O-Level Maths

Two papers, both with Part A and Part B.

### Paper I - 2 hours

Covers essential learning content only.

| Part | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Part A** | **25 short questions** (multiple choice) |
| **Part B** | **5 structured essay questions**, covering number, measurement, sets, probability and statistics |

Marking weights: knowledge and skills 50%, communication 30%, identifying
relationships 20%.

### Paper II - 3 hours 10 minutes

| Part | What |
| --- | --- |
| **Part A** | 3 algebra questions |
| **Part B** | 3 geometry questions |

Students **answer 5 questions: 3 chosen from Part A and 2 from Part B** (from the
official structure: 8 structured and 4 essay questions, choose 5).

### Total and grading

**Total: 100 marks.**

| Marks | Grade | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 75-100 | **A** | Distinction |
| 65-74 | **B** | Very Good Pass |
| 50-64 | **C** | Credit Pass |
| 35-49 | **S** | Ordinary Pass |
| Below 35 | **W** | Fail |

**You need at least an S in all six compulsory subjects.** Failing one
compulsory subject fails the whole O-Level, however good the other five are.

> **Caution on exact boundaries:** two independent sources give the 50 and 35
> cut-offs above, but we did not find them in a primary Ministry document.
> Well-attested, not officially confirmed. Check with the DoE before
> publishing these as fact on our site.

## What A-Level does

Taken in Grades 12-13, after O-Level.

| Exam | Held | Results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A/L 2026 | 10 August - 5 September 2026 | Before end of 2026 |
| A/L 2025 | November-December 2025, disrupted tail to January 2026 | 31 March / 2 April 2026 |
| A/L 2027 | 3-28 August 2027 | - |

**A/L has moved in the opposite direction** to O/L, from November-December to
August-September. So O/L in December now happens *after* the A/L exam, which is
the reverse of the pre-2020 order.

## What past papers are actually good for

This matters, because students tend to treat them as the whole revision plan.

### They are excellent for

- **Knowing the actual difficulty.** Past papers show what "hard" really means
  in the exam.
- **Timing.** Paper II is 3 hours 10 minutes. Nothing else teaches that.
- **Marking schemes.** Seeing where the marks are is worth more than the
  answers.
- **Question patterns.** The same formats repeat year to year.

### They are poor for

- **Diagnosing what you do not know.** A wrong answer tells you nothing about
  why.
- **Improvement.** Doing more past papers without fixing anything produces
  the same score, a lot faster.

> **Research supports this.** Spacing and interleaving work far better than
> blocked massed practice, and a stack of past papers done in one sitting is
  about the most blocked practice possible.

### How to use them properly

1. **Do one paper timed, properly, under exam conditions.** No notes, no phone.
2. **Mark it against the marking scheme**, question by question. See where the
   marks actually went.
3. **Diagnose the cause of each error**, not just the location:
   - Concept not understood?
   - Method not known?
   - Sloppy arithmetic?
   - Misread the question?
   - Ran out of time?
4. **Practise that specific thing**, spaced over days.
5. **Do the next paper after a week or two**, not the same evening.

This is exactly what our diagnosis engine should be for.

## What this means for the site

### Practically

- **Never hard-code the exam date.** Pull from a maintained calendar, and let it
  be updated.
- **Build study schedules that count back** from the published date.
- **Marking schemes are more valuable than answers.** Students need to see where
  marks are earned.
- **The 2-hour / 3h10m split is a design constraint.** Practice should have a
  real-time mode at both lengths.

### The seasonal business consequence

O/L demand peaks before a December exam: roughly September to December.

**Grade 6 has no such spike.** Term tests in around February-March and
September-October, but nothing like an exam cliff.

For a part-time teacher, that means **Grade 6 is steadier income** and O-Level
is sharper but more seasonal.
