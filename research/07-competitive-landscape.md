# Who Else Is Doing This?

**Research date: 9 October 2026**

A look at every maths learning platform we could find, what they do, and where
the empty space is.

---

## The short answer

**Almost everything in Sri Lanka is an archive or a video. Almost nothing is
actually teaching.**

There are thousands of past papers online, hundreds of YouTube channels, and
a dozen tutoring directories. But there is **no adaptive practice engine** for
the Sri Lankan syllabus in any language. Nothing between a PDF and a teacher.

That gap is the whole opportunity.

---

## Category 1: Past paper archives

The most crowded category by far. Free, and useful.

| Site | What it has |
| --- | --- |
| **PastPapers WiKi** | O/L and A/L papers, marking schemes, term tests, textbooks. Organised by grade, medium and lesson number, with Sinhala terms explained in English. The biggest one. |
| **mathspapers.info** | 1,066 papers. Tag by grade, medium, province, zone, term, year, and even specific school. |
| **govdoc.lk** | Syllabus and textbook PDFs, plus school-specific term tests. |
| **iruonline.com** | Term test papers with answers, Grades 9-11. |
| **e-thaksalawa** (Government) | The official government learning platform. 208 Grade 10 Sinhala maths lessons, plus provincial papers. |

### What they do well

Free. Fast. Exactly what students want right before an exam. Already indexed by
Google.

### What they do badly

- They are **PDF graveyards.** You download, you read, you do not learn.
- **Technical quality is poor.** One competitor still has `xxxxx` in its meta
  description, on the live site.
- **Almost no teaching.** A PDF does not explain why a step works.
- **Tamil is nearly empty.** mathspapers.info has 643 Sinhala papers, 412
  English, and **10 Tamil** - against Tamil-medium being around a quarter of all
  schools.
- None of them link the three languages together properly, so Google cannot
  tell they are the same content.

### Should we compete here?

**No.** These sites are good at being an archive. Anyone with the PDFs can copy
them, and the government already hosts the official versions for free.

**But we should use them.** Every one of them is a source of free, correctly
matched questions for our practice bank.

## Category 2: YouTube

Huge, free, and genuinely well-made in Sinhala.

| Channel | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Hasaranga Amarasingha** | About 57,600 subscribers. Probably the biggest Sinhala maths name. |
| **Ganitha Ennatha** | O/L maths, Grades 5-11. Drives a site with 10,000+ students. |
| **Maths with Piyumi** | Sinhala medium, Grades 6-11. |
| **OL Maths LK** | Textbook lessons with a companion website. |
| **DILAN MATHS** | English medium Combined Maths. |
| **Plus dozens of smaller channels** | Maths Kingdom, Maths Doctor, Maths Crew, and more. |

### What they do well

Free. Sinhala. Real teaching. Some of these teachers are genuinely excellent.

### What they do badly

- **No practice.** You watch, you understand, and then you have not actually
  done anything.
- **You cannot check your understanding** while watching.
- **No idea what you have and have not learned.**
- Video is expensive in data. A student on mobile data watches fewer lessons.
- **YouTube is hard to search well** for one specific thing you got stuck on.

> **Important:** these teachers are not enemies. Several of them are the best
> maths teachers in the country. We should link to them, not fight them. If a
> student watches a Sinhala YouTube lesson and then practices with us, that is
> a perfect outcome.

## Category 3: Tuition directories

Sites where tutors list themselves. Many are ads, not working marketplaces.

| Site | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **panthi.lk** | The biggest. 8,473 O/L ads. Costs LKR 4,990/month per advert. |
| **slclasses.lk** | 1,411 classes. Unusually, it shows actual fees. Also filters by Cambridge and Edexcel. |
| **Edugo.lk** | Bilingual. No platform commission, payment direct to the tutor. |
| **patashala.lk** | Marketing-heavy. Claims 2,500+ tutors. Unverified. |
| **Iskolaya.lk, FindMyTutor, TeachSL.lk** | Smaller directories. |

Plus on classified sites: **yaka.lk, **ikman.lk**, and **panthi.lk**.

### What they do well

They match parents to tutors. They work, in a basic way.

### What they do badly

Every single one does the same thing:

```
Free listing  ->  WhatsApp message  ->  cash
```

**No booking. No scheduling. No payment handling. No record. No reviews. No way
to track whether learning is happening.**

### An honest doubt

Several of these sites show signs of being built and then abandoned. Templated
layouts, suspiciously clean numbers, implausibly neat inventories. We should not
assume they have the scale their marketing claims.

## Category 4: Online maths schools

These are the real competitors. Sri Lankan businesses doing proper online
teaching.

| Business | What they do | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **IMOS.lk** | Grades 6-11. Sinhala and English. 266-413 lessons per grade. Live classes plus recordings. Claims 3,682+ students. | LKR 1,500 - 2,000/month |
| **OnlineMaths.LK** | Hasaranga Amarasingha. YouTube plus live classes plus an LMS. Free paper classes on Saturday nights. | Varies |
| **Mathsonline.lk** | Grades 6-11, both media. Home tuition delivery. Clear hours-based pricing. | Varies |
| **Dreams Online** | Premium recorded plus Zoom. Bank transfer with receipt upload. | Varies |

**IMOS.lk is the closest thing to a direct competitor.** Same grades, same
subject, same price range, same country. They are good at this.

## Category 5: AI tutors

This is the newest category and it is moving fast.

| Product | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **SriAI** | LKR 1,000/month. English, Sinhala and Tamil. NIE syllabus, O/L and A/L, past papers. Sells on one specific thing: **works with a local bank transfer, no international card needed.** |
| **SisuAI** | AI tutors for 14 subjects in three languages. Photograph your homework, get a step-by-step solution. |
| **EduOne** | Scans and **grades your handwritten paper** using AI. Gives you back marks and feedback. |

### What they do well

- **They are cheap.** LKR 1,000/month.
- **They speak Sinhala and Tamil.** This is now table stakes.
- **They never sleep** and never get annoyed.
- They already solved the payment problem for local customers.

### What they do badly

- **They are generic AI with a Sri Lankan hat on.** They will happily teach you
  American maths content you do not need.
- **They cannot be trusted with answers.** SisuAI's Play Store reviews include
  "when I asked a question, it gave me the wrong answers."
- **No real mastery tracking.** You ask, it answers, you forget.
- **No diagnosis.** "Wrong" is not a diagnosis. *Why* it was wrong is.

### The lesson

If we build AI features, we must build them on top of our own syllabus structure
and our own practice engine, not on top of a generic chatbot.

> **A tutor who is wrong is embarrassing. A website that is confidently wrong is
> worse, because a student cannot tell.**

## Category 6: International platforms

Available in Sri Lanka, but not built for it.

| Platform | What it does | Price | Problem |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Khan Academy** | Free videos and practice. Strong research base. | Free | **Wrong syllabus. No Sinhala.** Tamil is a partial demo site. |
| **Mathspace** | Best adaptive practice we found. Step-by-step hints. | $8-15/student/year | US and Australian curriculum only. |
| **ALEKS** | Sophisticated mastery engine. | ~$50/year | US curriculum. Priced for the US. |
| **Brilliant** | Interactive courses. Very good for visual topics. | $13.49/month | Expensive by local standards. |
| **GeoGebra** | Free. Calculators, graph tools, practice problems. | Free | Not a teaching sequence. |
| **DreamBox** | Strong adaptive learning for younger children. | $12.95-19.95/month | K-8 only, US curriculum. |
| **SplashLearn** | Gamified, younger children. | $7.49/month | Too young and too shallow for Grades 6-11. |
| **BYJU'S** | Indian edtech giant. | - | **Collapsed.** Assets in insolvency, including GeoGebra. |

### Khan Academy deserves special attention

It is free, it has excellent research, and it has been proven to work **in
Sri Lanka specifically** (Weeraratne & Chin, 2018, found +0.20 SD gains in
Grade 9 maths).

But:

- It teaches the **US and Indian** syllabus. Not NIE.
- It has **no Sinhala site at all.** Tamil is only partially translated.
- Its biggest impact came when **a school scheduled it into the timetable.**
  Students who used it on their own got much less.

> **The lesson from Khan Academy is not "build videos." It is: get your work
> into the weekly timetable. Motivation alone does not work.**

## Where the empty space is

### 1. Nobody does adaptive practice on the NIE syllabus

Every platform either has the wrong curriculum (international) or has no
adaptivity (everything local).

### 2. Tamil medium is basically empty

10 Tamil papers out of 1,066. Against roughly a quarter of all schools being
Tamil-medium.

**This is a real gap.** It is also the harder business, because a smaller market
with parents who mostly want Tamil-medium tuition. We should treat it as a
mission decision, not a revenue one.

### 3. Nobody combines content with teaching

The government has 208 free Sinhala maths lessons, correctly mapped to the
syllabus. Every archive has the right questions.

**What is missing is the layer that connects them:** step-by-step explanations
that diagnose *where* a student went wrong and *why*.

### 4. Local payment is a real advantage

SriAI sells on one specific line: "ChatGPT needs an international card. I don't
have one."

**That is a genuine wall no international platform will build over.** It is also
already being competed for, so we should not build our plan around it.

### 5. Nobody shows parents anything

Every platform targets the student. Not one sends a parent a report saying
"your child has improved from 40% to 70% on fractions this month."

Parents pay the money. Parents decide. And parents are currently flying blind.

## What to copy, and what to avoid

### Copy

- **Mathspace:** hints and next steps *inside* the question, not behind a link
- **ALEKS:** only show topics the student is likely to get right. Mastery and
  calm come from the same rule.
- **Siyavula (South Africa):** free textbooks, zero-rated mobile data, practice
  that never repeats the same question twice. The closest model to ours that
  works anywhere similar.
- **Khan Academy:** the research discipline. Measure properly or do not claim.
- **GeoGebra:** free graphing and geometry tools.

### Avoid

- **BYJU'S:** what not to do. Massive spending, bought companies, no sustainable
  revenue. It went from a $22 billion valuation to an insolvency estate.
- **The archive sites:** technically careless, and easily copied.
- **Brilliant's streaks and leaderboards:** good for confident students, actively
  harmful for anxious ones. About 1 in 5 students has maths anxiety.
- **TikTok-style motivational content:** popular in Sri Lanka, and widely
  criticised as manipulative. It is a bad look to build on.

---

## One thing to keep in mind

**Third Space Learning already runs its operations centre in Sri Lanka** and
employs Sri Lankan maths graduates as professional one-to-one tutors for UK
schools, with 2.3 million recorded tutoring sessions.

They have already proved that Sri Lankan maths talent is world-class and that
one-to-one teaching works at scale.

That is a helpful validation and an uncomfortable warning: **the best Sri Lankan
maths teaching in the world is happening, and we are not part of it yet.**
